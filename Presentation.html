THE DESIGN CHALLENGE

A powerful algorithm that evaluators could not yet use

Dense, multidimensional data

SVD-PCA, Horn’s slope kernels and covariance metrics are hard to read for non-technical users.

Slow site evaluation

Judging a landing or analog site meant hours of cross-referencing GIS datasets.

From academic to product

The project needed a stakeholder-ready NASA exploration interface to compete at the hackathon.

OUR DESIGN IDEA

Make complex planetary science feel like mission control.

A dark, tactical interface where anyone can tune, filter and explore Moon and Mars analog sites, with every score visible as a card, a percentage or a bar.
